# Current Digital and Virtual-Assistant Tool Landscape

This is a maintained orientation list, not a claim that every learner must master every product. Tools are grouped by work outcome. Product access, features, pricing, data handling, and interfaces change; use official documentation before teaching platform-specific steps.

- AI assistance: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, Perplexity, Notion AI.
- Automation and integration: n8n, Make, Zapier, Pipedream, Power Automate, IFTTT.
- CRM and sales operations: GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho CRM, Pipedrive, Close, Apollo.io, LinkedIn Sales Navigator.
- Work and knowledge management: Notion, ClickUp, Asana, Trello, Monday.com, Airtable, Coda, Jira, Confluence.
- Communication and meetings: Slack, Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Google Meet, Loom, Calendly.
- Documents and data: Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Google Sheets, Excel, Forms, Typeform, Jotform.
- Design and content: Canva, Figma, Adobe Express, CapCut, Descript.
- Social media: Meta Business Suite, Buffer, Hootsuite, Later, Sprout Social, Metricool.
- Email and lifecycle: Mailchimp, Klaviyo, ActiveCampaign, Brevo, ConvertKit/Kit, HubSpot Marketing Hub.
- Commerce: Shopify, WooCommerce, TikTok Shop, Shopee, Lazada, Stripe, PayPal.
- Web and development: WordPress, Elementor, Webflow, Wix Studio, Squarespace, GitHub, Vercel, Cloudflare.
- Analytics and reporting: Google Analytics, Google Search Console, Looker Studio, Microsoft Power BI, Tableau, Hotjar.
- Customer support: Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk, Help Scout, Gorgias.
